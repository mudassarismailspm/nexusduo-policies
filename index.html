Depending on the specific App you use, we may collect minimal data to ensure proper functionality, improve user experience, and deliver relevant features.

We use the information we collect to:

Our Apps may integrate standard third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs) to facilitate analytics, ad delivery, or in-app purchases. These services operate under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob / Ad Networks (If Applicable): May collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID) to serve non-personalized or personalized advertisements.
  • Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics (If Applicable): Used to gather aggregate crash data and app usage stats to fix errors.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Retention and Account/Data Deletion

  • Local Data: Data saved locally on your device is deleted immediately when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.
  • Data Deletion Requests: If an App provides user account creation or cloud storage, you have the right to request the deletion of your account and personal data. You may contact us at the email address provided below to submit a data deletion request.

Unless explicitly stated in an individual App's store listing, our Apps are general audience applications. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child under 13, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

6. Security

We prioritize the security of your data. We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.
